Honest development roadmap

Progress without pretend percentages.

This is a public overview of the private development project—not a release schedule or promise that every proposed feature will ship.

Core manuscript workspace

Chapter controls, word counts, local saves, and initial review tools exist in development builds. Continued device and storage testing is required.

Prototype built

Backup, recovery, and author control

Local export/restore, snapshots, save safeguards, and consent-oriented design are being exercised through tests and owner rehearsals. External backups remain important.

Testing

Advanced writing companion

A separate experimental message composer and idea capture have been developed. Voice recognition is not yet physically verified, and a live AI provider is not connected to the editor.

Development

Future AI, voice, and professional exports

Optional conversational assistance, manuscript read-aloud, richer formatting, and publishing-oriented formats are aspirations under evaluation. Privacy and explicit author consent remain required.

Planned

Public availability

More Android, accessibility, reliability, and security work must happen before any public release or sales. No launch date or paid plan is announced.
