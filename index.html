02 / THE HEART BEHIND THE WORK

Every good idea has a reason behind it.
Mine has a name: Stacy.

Long before the websites, writing tools, and big plans, there was Stacy—the person who believed in me, stood beside me, and helped me find the courage to keep building.

We’ve shared a life, a lot of hard roads, and more than a few impossible dreams. The work I put into the world carries a piece of that story: a belief that technology should help real people, respect their choices, and make difficult things a little easier.

So yes, I care about how a product looks and how well it works. But I care just as much about the person using it. That’s the heart behind everything I build.

— Loren Barnhart
